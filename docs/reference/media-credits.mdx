The videos visible in screenshots are demonstration material. They are not bundled with VideoCull.

## Blender Open Movies

Some screenshots include frames or filenames from these Blender Foundation and Blender Studio films:

- [Big Buck Bunny](https://studio.blender.org/projects/big-buck-bunny/)
- [Caminandes: Gran Dillama](https://studio.blender.org/projects/caminandes-2/) and [Caminandes: Llamigos](https://studio.blender.org/projects/caminandes-3/)
- [Tears of Steel](https://studio.blender.org/projects/tears-of-steel/)
- [Sintel](https://studio.blender.org/projects/sintel/)
- [Charge](https://studio.blender.org/projects/charge/)

Copyright Blender Foundation and the credited film contributors. The material is used under the applicable Creative Commons Attribution license for each project.

VideoCull and StippieDot are not affiliated with or endorsed by Blender Foundation.

## Screenshot presentation

The screenshots were captured from the real VideoCull application. Some website and README images arrange or rotate those captures on a colored background. They do not add application features or replace the interface with a mockup.
