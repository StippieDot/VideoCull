Video Cull is a local-first desktop app for sorting large video libraries fast. Open a folder, scan everything, review with the keyboard, and send marked files to the Recycle Bin when you are done.

## Highlights

- Thumbnail strips generated with bundled FFmpeg and FFprobe.
- Grid view for fast triage across large folders.
- Fullscreen review mode with playback, scrubbing, bookmarks, and undo.
- Duplicate review with suggested keepers and manual overrides.
- Cache that survives rescans and can live centrally, per drive, or next to your files.
