Video Cull is a free and open-source Windows desktop app built specifically for culling, sorting, and cleaning up large collections of local video files. Point it at a folder, and it scans the files, generates a strip of thumbnails for each video, and gives you a keyboard-driven workflow to decide what stays and what goes.

Nothing is uploaded. Nothing needs an account. Nothing is permanently removed until you confirm the delete step.

The core workflow is straightforward: open a folder, let thumbnails generate in the background, browse the grid to get a quick visual read on each clip, enter review mode for a closer look, make a decision, and move on. When you are ready, one command sends everything marked for deletion to the Recycle Bin when possible.

Video Cull runs entirely offline. There is no account, no cloud sync, and no data sent anywhere. It installs per user on Windows and does not require administrator rights.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a folder">
    Drag a folder onto the app window or press `Ctrl+O` to open the directory picker. Video Cull scans the folder and finds the video files inside it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate thumbnails in the background">
    FFmpeg and FFprobe are bundled with the app, so Video Cull can start extracting frames immediately. You can begin reviewing before every thumbnail finishes. Thumbnails appear as they complete and are stored in local cache.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse the grid">
    Each video appears as a card with a thumbnail strip, so you can identify the content at a glance without opening every file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and decide">
    Stay in the grid or switch to review mode to go through videos one at a time. Use the keyboard to mark each file as Keep, Delete, or Skip.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Commit the delete batch">
    Press `Ctrl+Backspace` to send everything marked for deletion to the Recycle Bin. Until you do, every decision is reversible.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key features

**Thumbnail strips** let you see frames sampled from different points in the file before opening anything. You can configure how many frames are generated per video and how far into the file the first frame is taken.

**Keyboard-driven decisions** keep review fast. In review mode, `K` keeps a video, `D` marks it for deletion, `S` skips it, and `Z` undoes the last decision. Shortcuts are customizable in Settings.

**Grid batch selection** lets you `Shift`-click a visible range and apply Keep, Delete, Skip, Reset, or thumbnail regeneration without opening each card.

**Recycle Bin safety** means deleted files go to the Windows Recycle Bin when possible, not straight to permanent deletion.

**Bookmarks** let you drop timestamped markers while a video is playing and return to them later.

**Duplicate review** helps you find likely duplicate files with pHash or visual similarity matching and work through suggested keepers instead of starting from scratch.

**Cache controls** let you choose where progress is stored and whether stale cache data or empty folders should be cleaned up automatically.

**Multi-folder sessions and reports** combine related folders into one queue and export either the full session or the current filtered result as HTML.

<Note>
  Video Cull runs locally. Your files and review decisions stay on your machine.
</Note>
