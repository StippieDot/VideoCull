Review mode is the one-at-a-time workflow for clips that need a closer look. It combines a full thumbnail strip, video metadata, and an in-app player with keyboard-first controls.

![Review mode](/screenshots/V2.0.0%20-%20ReviewMode.png)

## Core controls

- Press `K` to keep the current video.
- Press `D` to mark it for deletion.
- Press `S` to skip it.
- Press `Z` to undo the last decision.
- Press `Enter` to play in-app or `Ctrl+Enter` to open the file in your external player.

After a decision, Video Cull can move straight to the next clip so you can stay in flow.

## Playback and navigation

- Scrub through the video inside the app.
- Change playback speed with `[` and `]`.
- Use `Left` and `Right` to navigate, or scrub 5 seconds while a video is playing.
- Jump to the next undecided video instead of revisiting clips you already handled.

## Metadata and bookmarks

Review mode also keeps the current video's context close at hand:

- Ratings and favorites can be changed directly from the review screen.
- Bookmarks can be dropped while the video is playing and revisited later.
- Layout adapts to normal, maximized, and high-resolution displays.

<Note>
  If the built-in player should not handle a file, use the external player handoff instead of forcing playback in-app.
</Note>
